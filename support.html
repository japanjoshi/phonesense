Support

Help with PhoneSense on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Can't find your answer here? Write to info@oneprisminnovations.com. Say which device you use, and we'll get back to you.

There's more help inside the app too: Settings → Help.

The pause

The pause didn't come up

Open Settings → PhoneSense status in PhoneSense. It shows anything that's stopping the pause, and how to fix it. The usual causes:

  • iPhone and iPad: Screen Time isn't allowed for PhoneSense. Allow it from PhoneSense status, or in your device's Settings → Screen Time.
  • Android: the PhoneSense Accessibility service has been switched off, often by a battery saver. Turn it back on in your phone's Settings → Accessibility, and let PhoneSense run without battery limits.
  • It isn't night yet. The pause only comes up from your wind-down until your night ends. You can check the times in Settings, under Your night.
  • The app isn't one of the ones you chose. On the free plan, after the first week, two of your apps are paused: the two you've been paused at most.

Will I ever be locked out?

No. You can always go in from a pause. PhoneSense helps you decide; it never stops you.

Changing the time, or the apps

Open Settings in PhoneSense: Phone down by, Wind-down and Night ends at set your night, and Apps changes which apps are paused. A change made during the night starts the next night, so tonight's goal stays the way you set it.

Purchases

Restore a purchase

Bought PhoneSense Pro on another device, or reinstalled the app? Open Settings → PhoneSense Pro and tap Restore a purchase. There's no account: Pro follows the Apple Account or Google account you paid with, so make sure you're signed in to the same one.

Cancel or change a subscription

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PhoneSense.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → PhoneSense.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. If you're on the yearly plan's free trial, we remind you two days before it ends.

Refunds

Apple and Google handle payments, so refunds go through them: on Apple devices at reportaproblem.apple.com, and on Android through Google Play's order history. We can't issue refunds ourselves, but we're glad to help if something's gone wrong.

Your data

Your nights and your answers stay on your device; there's no account. In Settings:

  • Export my data saves a copy of everything PhoneSense has stored.
  • Delete all data erases it. Uninstalling PhoneSense does too.

Moving to a new phone? Your history stays on the old one, so export it first if you'd like a copy. Pro moves with you: restore it on the new phone.
