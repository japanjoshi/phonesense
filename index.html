Questions

Before you try it

Will I ever be locked out of an app?

No. You can always go in from a pause. PhoneSense helps you decide; it never stops you.

Does it read my screen or my messages?

No. On iPhone it uses Apple's Screen Time and never learns which apps you chose. On Android it only notices which app opened, and when. It can't see what's on your screen or what you type.

Does it work all day?

Only at night: from your wind-down, before the time you'd like your phone down by, until the morning. In the day, your phone is yours.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or later, and Android phones with Android 10 or later.

Is it a parental control app?

No. PhoneSense is for people managing their own phone, and makes no medical claims.
