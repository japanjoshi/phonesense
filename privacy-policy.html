PhoneSense

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026 · Applies to PhoneSense for iPhone, iPad and Android

PhoneSense helps you put your phone down earlier at night. To do that it has to know a little about your nights, and it keeps that on your phone. This policy says exactly what PhoneSense stores, the little that leaves your device, and the choices you have.

  • There is no account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • What PhoneSense learns about your nights stays on your device. We don't have a server that receives it.
  • PhoneSense never reads what's on your screen, what you type, or anything inside your apps.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your information. The only data that leaves your device is what's needed to handle a purchase, if you make one.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time, in Settings.

Who we are

PhoneSense is published by OnePrism LLP (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at info@oneprisminnovations.com.

What PhoneSense stores on your device

All of the following is kept only on your phone or tablet, inside PhoneSense's own private storage:

The detailed record of each night is deleted automatically after 90 days. Nightly summaries, weekly reports and your settings are kept until you delete them or uninstall PhoneSense.

Android

  • Accessibility service. PhoneSense uses Android's Accessibility service for one purpose: to notice when one of the apps you chose comes to the front, so it can show the pause. It is set up to receive only window-change events, with screen-content reading switched off (canRetrieveWindowContent is false). It learns which app is open and when. It cannot read your screen, your messages, or what you type.
  • Usage access (optional). If you allow it, PhoneSense reads which apps you used late at night, to suggest which ones to choose and to measure your nights more accurately. This also stays on your phone.
  • The apps you chose are stored by their package name (for example com.example.app).
  • PhoneSense's data is excluded from Google's cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it never leaves your phone that way either.

iPhone and iPad

  • Screen Time. PhoneSense uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls), with permission you give for your own device. You choose apps in Apple's own list. Apple gives PhoneSense opaque tokens in return, so PhoneSense never learns which apps you chose, or what you do in them.
  • The pause is drawn by Apple's Screen Time shield. PhoneSense only learns that a pause came up, when, and which button you tapped.
  • Like other apps' data, PhoneSense's data may be included in your own iPhone or iPad backups (in iCloud or on your computer), which Apple protects. We can't access them.

What leaves your device

Only what's needed to sell and check a subscription, if you choose to buy PhoneSense Pro:

  • Apple or Google take your payment, under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details, your Apple Account or your Google account.
  • RevenueCat, the service we use to keep track of whether your subscription is active, receives a random identifier created for this install (not your name or email), the details of your purchases and subscription (product, price, currency, dates, and the store's receipt), and technical details any app sends when it connects to a service: the app's version, your device model and operating-system version, your storefront country, and your IP address. RevenueCat processes this on our behalf; see RevenueCat's privacy policy.

None of this includes anything about your nights, the apps you use, the answers you give, or your reports. PhoneSense contacts RevenueCat when it opens, to check whether you have Pro, even if you've never bought anything; that check sends only the random identifier and technical details above.

PhoneSense contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software, and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Sharing

We don't sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to profile you. Apart from the purchase details above, we have nothing to share. We would disclose information only if the law required it, and the only information we could reach is what RevenueCat holds for your random identifier.

Your choices and rights

  • See and export your data: Settings → Export my data saves a copy of what PhoneSense has stored, as a file you can keep or share.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Delete all data erases everything PhoneSense has stored on your device. Uninstalling PhoneSense also removes it.
  • Turn things off: you can turn off notifications in PhoneSense or in your device's settings, and withdraw Screen Time, Accessibility or Usage access in your device's settings at any time. PhoneSense can't pause you without them.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel them in your App Store or Google Play account.
  • Purchase records: to ask for a copy of, or the deletion of, what RevenueCat holds about your purchases, contact us. Because there's no account, tell us roughly when you bought, from which store, and on which device, and we'll find the record.

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, or California), you have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict how it's used. Almost everything PhoneSense knows is on your device, where you already control it. For anything else, contact us and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Where the law asks for a legal basis: we handle purchase information to provide the subscription you bought (performing a contract), and the check for Pro to provide the app you're using (our legitimate interest in delivering what you paid for).

Children

PhoneSense is meant for people managing their own phone. It is not a parental-control app and is not directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children; it doesn't collect personal information from anyone beyond the purchase details above.

Security

Your data is kept in PhoneSense's private storage, which your device's operating system protects and encrypts when your device is locked. Connections to RevenueCat, Apple and Google are encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If what PhoneSense collects or sends changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app, and say so in the app's release notes.
